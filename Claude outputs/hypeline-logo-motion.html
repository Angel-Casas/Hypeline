The mark on hover, five ways

Hover each mark (the big one or the one in the rail). Every motion is short, plays once, and comes from something the app does: a peak rising, silk flowing, the thread being traced, the In/Out handles closing on a moment, the live heartbeat. All respect prefers-reduced-motion.

01 Rise peak grows

The crossbar swells into a taller spike — a moment of hype — and settles back when the pointer leaves. Quiet, and the most "on message". Needs the CSS d property (Chrome, Edge, Firefox; Safari falls back to no motion).

02 Shimmer silk flows

The gradient slides along the crossbar once, peach to butter, like light moving along the thread. Nothing changes shape, so it is the least distracting at rail size; reads as "alive" rather than "reacting".

03 Trace thread drawn

The crossbar redraws itself from the left stem to the right, the way the heatmap fills in while chat loads. A half-second of ink-and-silk handwriting; works in every browser.

04 Handles In · Out close

The two stems nudge inward and the peak tightens — the clip range closing on a moment. The only one that moves the letter itself; playful, and the closest to what the user actually does with the handles.

05 Beat live pulse

One heartbeat — the same rhythm as the live dot — with a soft silk halo that expands and fades behind the mark. The most "button-like" of the five; it tells the user the mark is clickable.

If you want a blend: 01 + 02 (rise while the silk flows) is the richest that still stays quiet; 03 alone is the most elegant; 05 is the clearest affordance. Whichever you pick goes into Logo.vue as a hover prop so the rail can use it and the icon elsewhere stays still.
