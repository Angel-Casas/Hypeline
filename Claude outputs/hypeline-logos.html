The H Spine, six ways

The original (02) stays as the reference. Five variations follow, each changing one idea: the letter's feet, which part carries the silk, whether the thread runs through, whether a pin stands on the peak, and how it sits as an app icon. Same three sizes as before, day and night.

02 The H Spine reference

The original: two heavy ink stems, a silk peak for a crossbar. Kept exactly as it was.

02·a Serif feet Gloock

The stems get the wordmark's slab feet and heads, so the mark and the Gloock "Hypeline" on the landing page are visibly the same letter. Tapered, slightly thinner stems; the peak stays silk.

02·b Inverted silk stems

The colour swaps sides: the stems wear the silk (vertical, peach at the top to sky at the foot) and the peak is ink. More colour in the rail, and the ink peak is the crisper shape at 16 px.

02·c Thread through timeline

The crossbar does not stop at the stems: the thread runs in from the left as a hairline, rises into the peak between the stems, and runs out to the right. The H becomes a window onto a longer timeline, which is what the dashboard is.

02·d Pinned moment

A pin stands on the peak — the moment marker from the heatmap — so the letter also tells the story: two handles, a peak, a pin. The stems are a touch shorter to give the pin headroom inside the same square.

02·e On the tile app icon

The H Spine set on a rounded paper tile with the silk hairline ring the rail and player already wear. This is the form for the PWA / home-screen icon; in the rail it is the same mark without the tile, so both can coexist.

Notes: 02·a is the one that ties back to the wordmark; 02·c reads most like "timeline" and works as a wide lockup too (the hairlines can extend under the word "Hypeline" on the landing page); 02·d carries the most meaning but is the busiest at 16 px; 02·e is not an alternative so much as the icon form of whichever you pick. 02·b is the boldest in the rail.
