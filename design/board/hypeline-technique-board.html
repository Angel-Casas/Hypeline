A Gradient mesh + film grain

Atmosphere instead of a flat fill.

E Magnetic buttons + custom cursor

The button leans toward you.

Within a radius the CTA follows the pointer a few pixels and the cursor dot swells into a soft disc. Small, but it is the difference between a page and a place.

  • cuberto.com
  • activetheory.net

F Frosted glass over moving colour

Panels that belong to the background.

Instead of white cards floating on pastel, the input sits in a frosted pane: blur and saturation of whatever drifts behind it, a one-pixel highlight on top. This is the natural home for the VOD input.

  • linear.app
  • apple.com (macOS pages)
