Hypeline · silk ring

Five ramps, no white in any of them

The white came from four pale tints — and they were there to solve a real problem: the palette skips green, so a one-way loop has to get from butter back to sky, and sRGB drags #ffde68 → #8eb0ff straight through a drab khaki. Each option below solves that a different way. Every one is the real ring CSS at the real speed.
